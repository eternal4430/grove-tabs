Keyboard

Ctrl+Shift+E
Open or close the panel
↑ ↓ Home End
Move between tabs
← →
Collapse / expand, or go to parent / first child
Enter
Switch to tab, or reopen a closed one
Delete (Mac: ⌘⌫)
Close tab (Shift closes its children too)
Alt+↑ ↓
Move tab among its siblings
Alt+→ ←
Indent under the tab above / outdent
N
New child tab (on a group header: new tab in the group)
G or Alt+G in search
Put the tab (and its children) in a new group
Shift+F10 or right-click
Tab menu: add to a group, remove from group, close; on a group: rename, colour, move, ungroup
/ or start typing
Search
Esc
Clear search, then close the panel

Change the panel shortcut, or add one for Houdini mode, at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. If another extension already uses Ctrl Shift E, Chromium won’t give it to Grove until you free it there.
